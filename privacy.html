Privacy Policy

Arcane Checkers · Effective June 25, 2026

This policy describes how Arcane Checkers (“the app”, “we”) handles information when you play on the web, as an installed app, or through the Google Play version of the game.

Information we collect

If you create an account, we collect and store:

  • Email address — used to sign in and recover your account
  • Username and display name — shown in your profile and in PvP matches
  • Game data — decks, card collection, adventure progress, gems, quests, cosmetics, and match-related data needed for cloud save and PvP
  • Authentication data — account credentials are handled by our auth provider; we do not store your password in plain text

You can play some features only after signing in. We do not require a postal address, phone number, or payment information for the free version of the app.

How we use information

  • Provide sign-in and cloud save across devices
  • Run player-vs-player matches and display opponent display names
  • Maintain your profile, collection, and game progress
  • Protect the service from abuse and fix bugs

Where data is stored

Account and game data are stored using Supabase (hosted cloud database and authentication). Data is transmitted over HTTPS.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell your personal information
  • We do not use your data for third-party advertising
  • We do not collect precise location from your device

Local data on your device

The app may store small amounts of data on your device (for example, session tokens, settings, or match checkpoints) so the game can load faster and resume after you switch apps. You can clear this by removing the app or clearing site data in your browser.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

Data retention and deletion

We keep your account data while your account is active. You may request account deletion by contacting us (see below). We may retain limited records if required by law or for fraud prevention.

Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. The effective date at the top will change when we do. Continued use of the app after changes means you accept the updated policy.

Contact

Questions or deletion requests: GitHub Issues on the Arcane Checkers repository.
